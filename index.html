Real people helping real people

A free board for volunteers who care and want to help, and for people, carers and families who would like a hand without going through the NDIS. Mainly Newcastle and the Hunter, open anywhere in Australia.

How it works

  1. Post a volunteer profile or a request. Only your first name and suburb are shown.
  2. Browse the board and send a connect request with a short message.
  3. Contact details stay hidden until both of you agree to share them.
  4. Meet in a public place first. No money changes hands.

Safety and guidelines

This is not an emergency service. In an emergency call 000. For crisis support call Lifeline 13 11 14 (24 hours).

For everyone

  • Meet in a public place first, like a library, cafe or shopping centre. Tell a friend where you're going.
  • No money handling. Volunteers never hold cash, cards, PINs or bank details, and never pay for things on someone's behalf.
  • Only share contact details when you're ready. The board keeps them hidden until both of you agree.
  • Keep health details and other people's names off the board.
  • You can say no or stop at any time.

For volunteers

  • Volunteers here are not NDIS providers and are not paid. This is informal, community help.
  • We recommend a Working With Children Check and a police check, plus first aid if you can.
  • The "checks" badge is self-declared. Coffeebreak does not verify checks. Ask to see them before you start.
  • Use your own car insurance if you drive someone, and stay within what you're comfortable doing.

Report a concern

Every listing has a Report a concern button. Reported listings are hidden straight away on your device.

If someone is in danger call 000. To report a crime that isn't urgent call Police Assistance Line 131 444.
